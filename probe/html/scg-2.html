Riftbound: League of Legends TCG

Riftbound is an action-packed trading card game featuring officially licensed characters from League of Legends. Send your Chosen Champion and other Units out to conquer and hold Battlefields. Score eight points while holding a Battlefield or conquering them all, and you win!

Shop Star City Games for Riftbound sealed products and singles. Compete in Riftbound at an SCG CON near you!
